If there are occupational hazards over my abilities, it's ADHD. Naturally
there are a lot of inconviniences that will cost me over time - that even
when it's fun drawing as you learn a new thing, it can still be difficult
to comprehend it's every geometry of it. Because the human body is
complex, memorizing for the first time involves following a guide as you
draw by multitasking; keeping it in mind of them having layers by muscles
as you stack all of them together, and once you begin to render with
the skin; you situate where you intend to brighten or darken up a part.
It's a lot of work indeed but I can then finally express different
poses with ease. I can confidently say I can recall visually the other
parts of the body, but it's often distinctive compared to reality and by
its sizes, that even when I try to embrace my failures it all still
prevents me from keeping on going as it's hard to instinctively
recognize the parts which I'ven't yet nailed it in my head. Being
unhappy about it can always hinder from how expressive I wanted it to be
so I have barely regained my mojo until the other day I came to the
conclusion that the bones are very vital to know, which I could've done
sooner in order to succeed making it right. Not to mention the tendons,
and where to start expanding the veins. Don't want to repeatedly fix
them errors forever. Figuring them out would even be more confusing - as
much as doing it with some logical shortcuts like Loomis Method,
modernly constructing the proportions with measurements, which are all
anathema to me that made me feel getting worse.
