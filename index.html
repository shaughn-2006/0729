As for my credentials, I'm barely self-disciplined. I only draw when I'm
inspired, but not inspired most of the time so there's only been one of
my works you'll see here that have been completed. I'm primarily a
sketcher and mostly used pens, but I've been motivated to use traditional
colors which the period of Renaissance/Baroque masters had access to and
wanna do more detailed paintings: the face in general is pretty smooth so
the shadows and light will "evaporate like vapor" - one of the reasons
why Leonardo is famous.
